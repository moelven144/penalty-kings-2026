## Description

Step up. Pick your corner. Hold your nerve.

Penalty Kings is the penalty shootout game you play with your friends. Swipe to shoot, bend it into the top corner, then switch sides and dive to save. Every shootout ends with a clip and a challenge link: your friend faces your exact five kicks, then takes theirs.

100% FREE. FOR REAL.
No ads. No purchases. No energy bars. Nothing to buy, ever. Everything in the game is earned by playing.

SWIPE TO SHOOT
• One swipe sets direction, height, power and curve
• Bend it, blast it, or chip a cheeky Panenka down the middle
• Hit it too hard and it flies over the bar

DIVE TO SAVE
• Read the striker's run-up, then swipe to dive
• Go early and you're guessing; go late and you won't reach the corner
• A fair keeper: no rigged saves, ever

CHALLENGE YOUR FRIENDS
• Send your five penalties as a link. They can play it straight away, even without the app
• Share a replay of your best goal or save
• Rematch. Repeat. Settle it.

PLAY FOR YOUR NATION
• 48 national teams
• The Nation League: every goal you score counts for your country's weekly ranking
• A new Daily Challenge every day, the same match for everyone in the world

ROAD TO GLORY
• 300 levels across five regions, with three clear stars to earn in each
• Target practice, bending shots, keeper trials and sudden death
• Unlock balls, kits, gloves, celebrations and stadiums by playing

Plays offline. Made for one thumb.

Penalty Kings is an independent game and is not affiliated with any football federation, league, club or tournament.
